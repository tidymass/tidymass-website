My primary research focuses on plant metabolomics data mining and bioinformatics tool development. Key projects include:

- Developing and maintaining the TidyMass shinyapp;  

- Elucidating the molecular mechanisms of flavonoid-mediated regulation in cotton fiber development and stress adaptation (*Gossypium hirsutum*);  

- Deciphering the metabolic basis and molecular pathways underlying functional lipid improvement in maize kernel;  

- Constructing comprehensive metabolite databases for major agroeconomic crops.
